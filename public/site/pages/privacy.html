Privacy policy

Draft for owner and legal review. Last updated 24 October 2026. This page describes the public website as built. It is not a certified legal opinion.

Who we are

TEA AMO is a café in Gokarna, Kathmandu, Nepal. This public website shows the live menu and how to visit or contact the café.

What this website does not collect

The public site does not take customer orders, delivery requests, or customer accounts. It does not ask guests for a name, phone number, or delivery address, and it does not keep a shopping cart.

Staff sign-in

Staff and administration sign in on a separate page. That login is for people who work at the café. It is not a customer account.

Cookies

This public site does not add analytics cookies and does not store a cart in the browser. The café’s hosting provider may keep ordinary connection logs needed to run the site.

Menu and business details

Menu names, prices, photographs, opening hours and contact details are published from the café’s own settings. Menu photographs are the ones saved with each item.

Older orders

Orders already taken at the counter, and any guest orders placed before public checkout was turned off, stay in the café’s records for operations and tax. Those records are not a public tracking page.

Your requests

Write or call the café using the contact details published on About & Contact. If those fields are empty, ask in person at TEA AMO, Gokarna. Complaints about privacy can also be raised with the relevant authority under Nepalese law, including the Privacy Act, 2075.
