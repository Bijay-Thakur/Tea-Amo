Privacy policy

Draft for owner and legal review. Last updated 24 October 2026. This page describes the public website as built; it is not a certified legal opinion.

Owner review needed: publish a phone/email, confirm retention days, and add PAN/VAT or registration only if they are real.

Who we are

TEA AMO is a café in Gokarna, Kathmandu, Nepal. This website lets guests view the live menu and place a one-time order.

What we collect

For a guest order we collect the name and phone number you type, and for delivery the address, optional landmark, and optional instructions. We do not create a customer account, saved address book, or marketing list.

Why we collect it

Staff need those details to accept, prepare, hand over or deliver the order, and to contact you about substitutions, delay, or payment. Financial totals may be kept for tax and accounting even after contact details are removed.

Who sees it

Authorized TEA AMO staff and the owner. The site is hosted with the café’s existing cloud provider (Supabase/PostgreSQL and the Vercel application host). We do not sell checkout details.

Cookies

The public site uses an essential cart stored on your device. It does not store your name, phone, or address. No extra analytics cookies are added by this application.

Retention

Order contact details are kept for fulfillment and a limited operational period (default 90 days after a closed order, unless the owner sets another period that still meets Nepal’s Privacy Act, 2075 and tax rules). After that they are redacted. You can ask about your order using the reference and the phone used at checkout.

Your requests

Write or call the café using the published contact details. If those fields are empty, ask in person at TEA AMO, Gokarna. Complaints about privacy can also be raised with the relevant authority under Nepalese law.
