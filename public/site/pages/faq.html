Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Browse and order as a guest. You will enter your name and phone for each new order.

Are the prices the café prices?

Yes. The website reads the same menu the POS uses. If a price changes before you confirm, checkout will ask you to review the cart.

Can I pay online?

This website accepts pay at pickup and cash on delivery. Staff can still collect eSewa, Fonepay or other café methods when they fulfill the order.

How do I cancel?

Call the café as soon as possible. Prepared food may not be cancellable. See the delivery policy.
