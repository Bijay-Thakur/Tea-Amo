Delivery, cancellation and refunds

Draft for owner and legal review. Last updated 24 October 2026.

Owner review needed: set delivery fees, service area, and the support phone before launch.

Service area

Delivery is only offered when the owner has enabled it and only to the areas the café has configured. We do not promise delivery across Kathmandu.

Fees and estimates

The delivery fee and any minimum order are shown at checkout from the café’s settings. Preparation time is an estimate only when the owner has entered one.

Pickup

Collect the order at TEA AMO, Gokarna, Kathmandu. Bring the order reference and the phone used at checkout.

Cancellation

Call the café immediately. If kitchen work has started, cancellation may not be possible. Staff will say what can still be stopped.

Wrong, missing or poor-quality food

Contact the café with the order reference. TEA AMO will review the complaint. Remedies can include replacement, a partial refund, or another fair remedy required by Nepalese consumer-protection law. We do not claim an unconditional no-refund rule for perishable food.

Refunds

Refunds, if due, follow the payment method used with staff (cash or the café’s accepted methods). Website checkout does not take card or wallet charges itself.
