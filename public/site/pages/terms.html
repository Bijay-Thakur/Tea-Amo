The website

This website lets you browse TEA AMO’s menu and find the café in Gokarna, Kathmandu. It does not accept customer orders, delivery requests, or customer accounts.

Prices and availability

Prices are in Nepalese rupees and come from the same menu the café uses at the counter. Availability can change during the day. An item is shown only when the café has marked it for sale. Paying and ordering happen at TEA AMO, not on this website.

Photographs

Item photographs appear when the café has saved one for that item in administration. Other photographs on the site are atmosphere pictures of tea.

Visiting

Opening hours, phone, email and social links come from the café’s published details. If a detail is missing, ask at the café.
